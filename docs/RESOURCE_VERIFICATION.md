## Consequential corrections

- Removed unsupported county emergency-rental opening claims, simulated waitlists, arbitrary $ awards, and the generic “Community Deposit Assistance” pseudo-program. Deposit help is now an explicitly uncertain inquiry through 211 or coordinated entry.
- Reduced the catalog from broad demo listings to source-backed entries. Omitted listings were not asserted to be closed; they were deferred because their program scope and current evidence were not strong enough for this pass.
- Added real program constraints: Duluth ZIP restriction and AVLF pending-filing service. `restrictionSourceUrl` is required before ZIP/filing/family metadata may exclude someone. Unsourced restrictions remain unknown.
- `getAllResources()` always uses this reviewed bundle. A stale Supabase table cannot silently restore demo openings. Generated seed SQL is an optional upsert artifact and does not delete old database rows.
- Services with a city boundary or complex referral/household rule remain provisional and say so. No fabricated ZIP-to-city table or complete eligibility rule was introduced.

No eviction impact statistic was added. AVLF publishes approximate filing/representation figures, but no specific measurement period was established in this review; they are intentionally not used as dated evidence of impact or completed evictions. No Hacklanta judging rule or winner claim was invented.

Before public service use, contact priority providers to confirm intake and review this catalog periodically. Providers make the eligibility decision. Source retrieval is not professional or agency certification.
